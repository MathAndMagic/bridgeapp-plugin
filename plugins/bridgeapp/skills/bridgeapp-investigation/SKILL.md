# Investigating

Find out what is going on and explain it with evidence. Change nothing while
you do: no code, tasks or documents. A fix is a separate step the user decides
on.

- **Search before you reason.** Tasks, threads, documents, and any other source
  you can reach — the code, its history, logs. The answer is often already in
  an older discussion nobody linked.
- **Think in timelines.** When did it last work, what changed since; for a
  decision, who decided what and when. A later decision replaces an earlier one
  only when it says so or comes from whoever owns the call.
- **Evidence over theory.** Every claim points to something you saw. What you
  did not verify is a hypothesis and is called one.
- **Don't stop at the first plausible answer.** It has to explain everything
  you found.

Report short: the answer with its confidence, the evidence with links, the
contradictions, what you ruled out, and what you suggest. If you cannot reach
a confident answer, say what would settle it.

Reply in the user's language.
